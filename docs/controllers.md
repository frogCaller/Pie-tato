# Controllers

Any USB or Bluetooth game controller (Xbox, PlayStation, 8BitDo, the cheap SNES-style ones), or a
GPi Case's own buttons. Plug them in any time.

- Hold Home and press Left to go from the apps to the console, where the buttons work as keys (Up for
  the last command, A for Enter), and press Home to come back. Home on its own never leaves the apps. To
  start in the console instead, set Settings > At start-up to Terminal (or Game, to start in the game you
  played last).
- If your controller is Bluetooth, put it in pairing mode and pick it in Settings > Bluetooth, or
  under Bluetooth on the web page. Keyboards and headphones pair the same way, and while headphones
  are connected, sound plays through them. If they disconnect, what's playing pauses. A USB speaker
  works the same way while it's plugged in.
- If A and B are the wrong way round, use that controller to set Settings > Swap A and B to On.
- If a button does the wrong thing, open Settings > Controller and press each button it asks for.
- If your controller has no Home button, press Select and Start together.
- In a GPi Case, if the case's buttons don't come on when it starts (it happens now and then),
  Pietato plugs the case's USB in again by itself after about 10 seconds. If they still don't come
  on, switch it off and on again.
- A keyboard works the apps too: W A S D (or the arrow keys) is the d-pad, Enter or Space is A,
  Backspace or Esc is B, X and Y are X and Y, Q and E are L and R, P is Start and O is Select. Home
  stays on the controller. In the console, and in a box you're typing into, it types as usual.
- A trackpad (like the BB9900's) or a mouse works too: swipe to move, one step for a short swipe and a
  few for a long one, and press it to pick. The right button goes back.

## Another Pietato

One Pietato's buttons can work another of yours, as if they were a controller plugged into it: a Pietato on
the TV that has no controller, say, from your GPi Case. On the one in your hand, open Home > Control another
Pietato and pick the other one with A. From then on your buttons are the other one's, held ones too, so its
games play as they should. The screen in your hand shows which one it's controlling and lights up the buttons
you press. To have your buttons back, hold Home (or Select and Start together) for two seconds.

- Both need Settings > Share with other Pietatos on, and it only works on your own network. If the other one's
  web page has a password, you type it once, the first time.
- If the WiFi drops for a moment, what you're holding is let go of over there, and it carries on when the WiFi's
  back.
- Like the web page's remote, it can't use the other one's terminal (Home brings its menus back) or answer your
  coding agent.
- Up to four can control the same one at once, each a player of its own in a game.
